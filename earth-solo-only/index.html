Weekly Cash for Coal Event Working

The idea

Coal is King! A weekly contest tied to the wipe cycle running from the start of wipe Thursdays at 3PM MST to Wednesday 7PM MST. Players convert Scrap into Coal at Outpost vending machines. That is the only way to get it. One coal costs 250 scrap. Player with the most coal at the end of the event wins.

Requirements

  • The event starts with $5 in the prize pool.
  • For the event to reach a payout, the server must reach 500 total hours played by everyone across the wipe. Miss that and there is no payment that week.
  • Donations made on my website will increase the payout. When donations hit $5, that raises the total prize pool to $10. That would raise the requirement of hours played to 1000 to pay this $10. Every 500 hours played activates another $5 from the prize pool.
  • If total prize pool is $40 and the total hours played is 700, that would be a $5 payout to the winner.
  • A banner in the top left of your screen shows the server's running hour total and how close it is to the next payout. It updates while you play, so you always know where the server stands.
  • If the 500 hours played goal is not met, the prize money is not lost. It carries over and is added to the following week's prize. So, if the prize pool is $40 and total hours played is 700, There would be a $5 payout and $35 is carried over to the next week's prize pool.
  • One more example. If the total hours of the server is 2200 and the prize pool is $10. It is only a $10 payout unless more money is donated. Remember though, a donation is not required to play and win.

Counting the Coal

Coal only counts toward your total once it's stored in a box or barrel you placed. This includes small boxes, large boxes and both barrels. That is it. Tool Cupboards, Lockers, Cabinets etc. will not be counted including coal carried in your inventory. The coal must be safe in a box for it to be counted. Take care of your coal! In addition, coal must be 10 meters or less from a Tool Cupboard you own. You cannot just hide a bunch of boxes randomly around the map unless close to your TC.

Server counts coal by matching the owner steam ID for each container (you placed it). A good example: if you raid a base and decide to authorize Tool Cupboard and make it a new home, make sure you take the coal out of the boxes, pick up the boxes and replace them so they carry your id, not the person you raided. Then put it in the box or barrel you placed.

How the reveal works

  • During the entire event you will only know how much coal you have. You can use the command /checkcoal in chat to see your total. Use /checkcoal after placing coal in a box to see if it is counted. Make sure the box is 10m or less from your TC.
  • You will never know how much coal anyone else has. This is by design.
  • Still planned: 60 minutes (maybe more) before the event ends the chat command /leaderboard opens up to everyone. This will show a live top 10 plus your own rank if you are outside the top 10.
  • The command /checkcoal opens up for everyone so you can see totals of all players.
  • Still planned: once active, the leaderboard refreshes every 10–15 seconds, then every 5 seconds once the countdown crosses the final 1-minute mark.

Build cost is 1,000 Metal Frags, 100 HQM, 1,000 Scrap. Use chat command /buildwb3 to build. Requires Phase 3 and standing within 10m of a placed Workbench Level 2.

Why use chat commands?

Vanilla workbench recipes are hard-coded to the crafting menu and need blueprint fragments to craft in that menu. Only work around is using a chat command.

Base building "privilege" is now smaller. The 10 meter cap is measured from where you're standing when you place an item, not from where the item ends up. Same readout shows while holding the Building Plan tool, tracking distance to your nearest Tool Cupboard.

Build freely with the Building Plan tool as long as you're standing within 10m of your TC and the overlay stays green. Tool Cupboards are locked in place. The first Tool Cupboard you put down in an area sets the center of that building area for the rest of the wipe. If it gets destroyed you can replace it, but it has to go back within half a meter of the same spot. Destroying your own Tool Cupboard to move it higher up your base or further out will not bring your build area with it, the placement is simply refused. Place your Tool Cupboards with care. If your base is completely gone, decayed or raided flat, the lock clears and you can build there again.

Cheaters Working

There is real money on the line here, and that attracts cheaters. Adding steps to make sure they cannot join.

  • Rust runs its own server side checks for flying, noclipping through walls, impossible melee hits and faked projectiles. Vanilla servers set this enforcement to 1. If it happens they get kicked from the server, but can load right back in, so it is basically useless.
  • Enforcement is set to level 2 on this server Level 2 bans them instead.
  • PUBLIC STEAM PROFILES ONLY. If your Steam profile or game details are set to private you cannot join.
  • Your Steam account gets checked the first time you connect. It has to be at least 90 days old and have at least 100 hours in Rust. You get bounced once with a message telling you to hit Connect again. That is the check running. It takes a couple of seconds and only happens once, ever.
  • You have to own Rust. Family shared copies are blocked.
  • Accounts with a VAC ban, or more than one game ban, in the last year are blocked. Older bans are ignored on purpose.
  • Steam trade banned accounts are blocked.

No Entry Fee (Freeroll) Explanation

I am based in Arizona. Arizona law defines gambling as risking something of value for the opportunity to win a benefit from a contest of chance or skill. Skill is written right into the statute, so proving this event takes skill would not change anything. The only thing that keeps this clean is that there is no entry fee, ever. Nothing of value is risked, so it never becomes gambling in the first place.

That means the prize is never pooled from player money. I fund it, with help from donations and from unrelated merchandise sold on this site. Donating or buying something gets you nothing in the event. No entry, no bonus, no advantage, no special treatment. Everyone plays on identical terms and anyone can win without ever spending a cent.
