Progression & workbenches Live

Slow progression is the core identity of the wipe. Three phases, each unlocking more.

Workbench Level 1 — vanilla menu

500 Wood, 100 Metal Frags, 50 Scrap. Craftable normally in the vanilla menu — this one still works as-is.

Workbench Level 2 — /buildwb2

500 Metal Frags, 20 HQM, 500 Scrap. Requires Phase 2+, and standing within 10m of a placed Workbench Level 1.

Workbench Level 3 — /buildwb3

1,000 Metal Frags, 100 HQM, 1,000 Scrap. Requires Phase 3+, and standing within 10m of a placed Workbench Level 2.

Why chat commands?

No Blueprint Fragments on this server, and the vanilla WB2/WB3 recipes hard-require them client-side — unbuildable through the normal menu no matter what. The commands are the only way to build them now.

Quarries Live rebuilt 2026-07-26

Just reworked. Quarries no longer trickle output steadily — they burst-fire on a pattern, then shut themselves off for that tank of fuel, same as running dry. Needs a manual restart to run again.

HQ Metal Quarry

Runs on a ~25-tick fuel cycle. Per phase:

  • Phase 1: 1 HQM on the first tick, nothing for the next 15 ticks, then 1 per tick until it's given 10 HQM total for that tank — then it stops.
  • Phase 2: 1 HQM per tick for the first 5 ticks, nothing for the next 5, then 1 per tick until 20 HQM total — then it stops.
  • Phase 3: 1 HQM per tick for the first 20 ticks, then 2 per tick until 30 HQM total — then it stops.

Sulfur Quarry & Stone Quarry (stone + metal ore)

No bursting — flat percentage of vanilla output, same numbers every tick: 20% in Phase 1, 40% in Phase 2, 60% in Phase 3.

  • Outpost's static Clan Table monument fixture and its chairs — on top of the crafting ban above, there's no clan hub to gather at either.
  • The Hunter NPC at the Large Barn monument — no longer spawns, so the Boar Hunt mission can never be picked up.

World & spawns Live

Single spawn point

Everyone spawns at the same beach location each wipe — whichever spot the very first respawn happens to land on gets locked in for the rest of the wipe. Resets fresh every wipe. Bag/bed respawns are untouched.

Junkpiles halved

Roadside junkpiles (barrel and car wreck spawns) are capped at 50% of the vanilla amount across the map.

Ground ore piles removed

Hand-pickup Metal Ore and Sulfur Ore piles that scatter on the ground are removed the instant they spawn. Only these two — the mineable rock nodes you hit with a pickaxe are untouched, as are Wood and Stone piles.

Weekly Coal cash event Planned — not built yet

A weekly contest tied to the wipe cycle. Nothing below is live — this is the locked-in design, waiting to be built.

The idea

Players convert Scrap into Coal at a vending machine (500 Scrap = 1 Coal). Coal has to be physically carried and stored — it's raidable on the way to the exchange and while held. Whoever holds the most Coal when the wipe ends wins the prize.

Why no entry fee

Arizona law treats a contest as gambling only if it has an entry fee, a prize, and chance, all three. Dropping the entry fee removes it from that definition entirely — Dan funds the ~$20 prize himself instead of pooling entry money.

How the reveal works

  • Admin-only /checkcoal the rest of the wipe — players can never see each other's totals during normal play.
  • Final ~30 minutes before wipe, the leaderboard opens to everyone: live top 10, plus your own rank if you're outside it.
  • Refreshes every 10–15 seconds normally, every 5 seconds once the countdown crosses the 1-minute mark.

Still undecided

  1. One Scrap→Coal exchange location, or several across the map?
  2. Leaderboard overlay always-on during the reveal window, or toggleable?
  3. Exact reveal window length — 30 minutes is the current thinking, not locked in.

Open issues Open / unconfirmed

Built but unverified, known gaps without a fix yet, or still waiting on a decision. Don't advertise these as guaranteed until they're checked off.

  • Corpse-in-safe-zone exploit: a player's own dead body inside a safe zone may work as free protected storage, since vanilla safe zones block others from looting it. Not yet addressed.
  • New Apartment Complex safe zone may be too large (city-block sized). A diagnostic tool exists to check this but hasn't been run yet.
  • Tech Tree Control's boat-block doesn't play the denial beep like everywhere else on the server. Waiting on a decision about editing that third-party plugin's source.
  • Wipe timing automation (Thursday 3pm) is set up but hasn't been separately confirmed to fire reliably or handle timezones correctly every week.
  • Minicopter 3-minute buyer lock is built but not tested with a second player yet.
  • Building privilege radius (more than one TC per base area) — research question only, not decided or built.
  • Coal at Outpost's newer Marketplace terminals — already added to the classic vending machines, not yet the newer terminal system.
