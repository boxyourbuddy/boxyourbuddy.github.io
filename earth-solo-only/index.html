Weekly Cash for Coal Event Working

The idea

Coal is King! A weekly contest tied to the wipe cycle running from the start of wipe Thursdays at 3PM MST to Wednesday 7PM MST. Players convert Scrap into Coal at Outpost vending machines. That is the only way to get it. One coal costs 250 scrap. Player with the most coal at the end of the event wins.

Coal only counts toward your total once it's stored in a box or barrel you placed. This includes small boxes, large boxes and both barrels. That is it. Tool Cupboards, Lockers, Cabinets etc. will not be counted including coal carried in your inventory. The coal must be safe in a box for it to be counted. Take care of your coal! In addition, coal must be 10 meters or less from a Tool Cupboard you own. You cannot just hide a bunch of boxes randomly around the map unless close to your TC.

Server counts coal by matching the owner steam ID for each container (you placed it). A good example: if you raid a base and decide to authorize Tool Cupboard and make it a new home, make sure you take the coal out of the boxes, pick up the boxes and replace them so they carry your id, not the person you raided. Then put it in the box or barrel you placed.

No Entry Fee (Freeroll) Explanation

I am based in Arizona, and Arizona law treats a contest as gambling only if it meets three conditions: it has an entry fee, a prize, and chance. All three of those equal gambling. Dropping the entry fee, or any of the other two, removes it from that definition. This is why I must fund the ~$20 prize myself instead of pooling entry money. However, if I can prove it takes more than chance to win the event I could allow for a small entry fee and we can raise more prize money. I think I will need to do this for a few weeks to be able to prove this. That is why I am taking away many of the chance items of the vanilla game and leaning more towards skill/grinding. Chance would mean just entering the event would give you a "chance" to win. I do not think that just by paying an entry means you have a chance to win this event, because you have to have to actually work slightly to get coal, build a base to keep it safe and survive the whole wipe with everyone wanting your coal. I know it takes more than chance, but I want some solid proof.

How the reveal works

  • During the entire event you will only know how much coal you have. You can use the command /checkcoal in chat to see your total. But you will never know how much anyone else has. This is by design.
  • Still planned: 60 minutes (maybe more) before the event ends the chat command /leaderboard opens up to everyone. This will show a live top 10 plus your own rank if you are outside the top 10.
  • The command /checkcoal opens up for everyone so you can see totals of all players.
  • Still planned: once active, the leaderboard refreshes every 10–15 seconds, then every 5 seconds once the countdown crosses the final 1-minute mark.

Still undecided

  • Leaderboard overlay always-on during the reveal window,toggleable or both?
  • There must be some requirements for the event to be valid, meaning a player will be paid. I was thinking of a 20 coal minimum and some kind of population average, or total hours played by everyone. For example if there are only 10 active players for the whole event, I would not qualify that as a valid event. No payment. It needs to be busy. That is the goal. Requirements will be known before the beginning of the event. I just need to get a better idea of interest. First event is still weeks away, so there is time.

No Teaming Working

This is an event server that has real money on the line. No teaming of any fashion will be tolerated. Period. Be nice to each other outside the game though.

  • This is a Solo Only server.
  • Kill on site or run away.
  • If you have questions ask in chat or on discord.
  • No giving items away. (base included)
  • No accepting items from others. (base included)

Basically, don't help other players. You shouldn't want to. It would be like passing the ball to the other team, or scoring a goal for the other team.

Progression & workbenches Working

Slow progression is the core identity of the wipe. It is supposed to be hard. You are going to have to choose what you want to do with your scrap. Research and raid to get your coal. Just grind as a naked and hoard coal in many places. A combinaton of both plus many other ways.

Workbench Level 1

Build cost is 500 Wood, 100 Metal Frags, 50 Scrap. No matter what the crafting menu says, make sure you have 50 Scrap in you inventory.

Workbench Level 2

Build cost is 500 Metal Frags, 20 HQM, 500 Scrap. Use chat command /buildwb2 to build. Requires Phase 2 and standing within 10m of a placed Workbench Level 1.

Workbench Level 3

Build cost is 1,000 Metal Frags, 100 HQM, 1,000 Scrap. Use chat command /buildwb3 to build. Requires Phase 3 and standing within 10m of a placed Workbench Level 2.

Why use chat commands?

Since there are no Blueprint Fragments on this server, and the vanilla WB2/WB3 recipes are hard-coded to the crafting menu, WB2/3 are unbuildable through the normal menu no matter what. The commands are the only way to build them now. Just type in chat /buildwb2 or /buildwb3 and it will appear in your inventory as long as you have the right items to craft in your inventory.

Quarries Working rebuilt 2026-07-26

Quarries no longer trickle output steadily, they output in a pattern and shut themselves off for that tank of diesel, same as running dry. Needs a manual restart to run again.

HQM Quarry

Runs on a 25-tick fuel cycle. Per phase:

  • Phase 1: 1 HQM on the first tick, nothing for the next 15 ticks, then 1 per tick until it's given 10 HQM total per diesel.
  • Phase 2: 1 HQM per tick for the first 5 ticks, nothing for the next 5, then 1 per tick until 20 HQM total, then it stops.
  • Phase 3: 1 HQM per tick for the first 20 ticks, then 2 per tick until 30 HQM total, then it stops.

Sulfur Quarry & Stone Quarry (stone + metal ore)

A flat percentage of vanilla output, same numbers every tick: 20% in Phase 1, 40% in Phase 2, 60% in Phase 3.

Base building "privilege" is now smaller. The 10 meter cap is measured from where you're standing when you place an item, not from where the item ends up. Same readout shows while holding the Building Plan tool, tracking distance to your nearest Tool Cupboard.

Build freely with the Building Plan tool as long as you're standing within 10m of your TC and the overlay stays green. You can still reach out and place a foundation/buildable a few meters farther while standing right at the 10m line, but that doesn't mean you'll be able to reach far enough after to build the wall on its far edge. Test placing walls before upgrading. Once you're more than 40m from any Tool Cupboard you own, that area counts as a new build and you can place foundations and a new TC.

Cheaters Working

There is real money on the line here, and that attracts cheaters. Adding steps to make sure they cannot join.

  • Rust runs its own server side checks for flying, noclipping through walls, impossible melee hits and faked projectiles. Vanilla servers set this enforcement to 1. If it happens they get kicked from the server, but can load right back in, so it is basically useless.
  • Enforcement is set to level 2 on this server Level 2 bans them instead.
