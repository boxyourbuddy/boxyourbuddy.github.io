Weekly Cash for Coal Event Working exchange + /checkcoal built & tested — leaderboard/reveal window still planned

A weekly contest tied to the wipe cycle. The Scrap→Coal exchange is live and tested. /checkcoal is live and built for both admins and regular players, but the player-facing side hasn't been tested with a second player yet (see Open Issues). The public end-of-wipe leaderboard reveal below is still design-only, waiting to be built.

The idea

Players convert Scrap into Coal at any Outpost vending machine (250 Scrap = 1 Coal). Coal only counts toward your total once it's stored in a box or barrel you placed, (small box, large box, and barrel-type containers all confirmed working) — Tool Cupboards, Lockers, and coal just carried in your inventory don't count. Server tallies by matching each container's owner steam ID, so if you raid a base and it has coal, you have to take it out and put it in a box you placed before it counts as yours. Whoever holds the most Coal when the event ends wins the prize.

Why no entry fee

I am based in Arizona, and Arizona law treats a contest as gambling only if it has an entry fee, a prize, and chance. All three of those equal gambling. Dropping the entry fee, or any of the other two, removes it from that definition entirely. I funds the ~$20 prize myself instead of pooling entry money. However, if I can prove it takes more than chance to win the event I could allow for a simple $1 entry fee and we can raise more prize money. I think I will need to do this for a few weeks to be able to prove this. That is why I am taking away many of the chance items of the vanilla game and leaning more towards skill/grinding. Chance would mean just entering the event would give you a "chance" to win. I do not think that just by paying a $1 entry means you have a chance to win, because you have to have 20 coal and survive everyone wanting to kill you to get there. I know it takes more than chance, but I want some solid proof.

How the reveal works

  • Live now: /checkcoal in chat works differently by role. Admins get everyone's stored Coal total for the whole server. Regular players get just their own total, usable any time.
  • Players can never see each other's totals during normal play until the reveal an hour before event ends.
  • Still planned: 60 minutes before the event ends, the chat command /leaderboard opens to everyone. Live top 10, plus your own rank if you're outside it.
  • Still planned: once active, the leaderboard refreshes every 10–15 seconds, then every 5 seconds once the countdown crosses the 1-minute mark.

Still undecided

  • Leaderboard overlay always-on during the reveal window,toggleable or both?
  • Exact reveal window length. Between 30 and 60 minutes before event ends is the current thinking.
  • There must be some requirements for the event to be valid, meaning a player will be paid. I was thinking of a 20 coal minimum and some kind of population average, or total hours played by everyone. If there are only 10 active players (not a real number) for the event, I would not qualify that as a valid event. No payment. It needs to be busy. That is the goal. Requirements will be known before the beginning of the event. I just need to get a better idea of interest. First event is still weeks away, so there is time.

Progression & workbenches Working

Slow progression is the core identity of the wipe. Three phases, each unlocking more.

Workbench Level 1

500 Wood, 100 Metal Frags, 50 Scrap.

Workbench Level 2

Build cost is 500 Metal Frags, 20 HQM, 500 Scrap. Use chat command /buildwb2 to build. Requires Phase 2 and standing within 10m of a placed Workbench Level 1.

Workbench Level 3

Build cost is 1,000 Metal Frags, 100 HQM, 1,000 Scrap. Use chat command /buildwb3 to build. Requires Phase 3 and standing within 10m of a placed Workbench Level 2.

Why use chat commands?

Since there are no Blueprint Fragments on this server, and the vanilla WB2/WB3 recipes are hard-coded to the crafting menu, WB2/3 are unbuildable through the normal menu no matter what. The commands are the only way to build them now. Just type in chat /buildwb2 or /buildwb3 and it will appear in your inventory.

Quarries Working rebuilt 2026-07-26

Quarries no longer trickle output steadily, they output in a pattern and shut themselves off for that tank of diesel, same as running dry. Needs a manual restart to run again.

HQM Quarry

Runs on a 25-tick fuel cycle. Per phase:

  • Phase 1: 1 HQM on the first tick, nothing for the next 15 ticks, then 1 per tick until it's given 10 HQM total per diesel.
  • Phase 2: 1 HQM per tick for the first 5 ticks, nothing for the next 5, then 1 per tick until 20 HQM total, then it stops.
  • Phase 3: 1 HQM per tick for the first 20 ticks, then 2 per tick until 30 HQM total, then it stops.

Sulfur Quarry & Stone Quarry (stone + metal ore)

A flat percentage of vanilla output, same numbers every tick: 20% in Phase 1, 40% in Phase 2, 60% in Phase 3.

Live on-screen distance readout shows while you're holding a bag or bed, so you can see how close you are to the limit before placing.

Base building "privilege" is now smaller. The 10 meter cap is measured from where you're standing when you place an item, not from where the item ends up. Same readout shows while holding the Building Plan tool, tracking distance to your nearest Tool Cupboard.

Build freely with the Building Plan tool as long as you're standing within 10m of your TC and the overlay stays green. This doesn't limit how far the foundation/buildable itself can be placed, only where you're standing when you place it. You can still reach out and place a foundation/buildable a few meters farther while standing right at the 10m line, but that doesn't mean you'll be able to reach far enough after to build the wall on its far edge. Test placing walls before upgrading. Once you're more than 40m from any Tool Cupboard you own, that area counts as a new build and you can place foundations and a new TC.
