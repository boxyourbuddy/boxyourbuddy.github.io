Easy RSVP Book Reader

Easy RSVP Book Reader is a free, native macOS speed reading app that flashes one word at a time, using a technique called Rapid Serial Visual Presentation (RSVP) with ORP, Optimal Recognition Point, alignment. Drop your EPUB files directly into the app for a smooth book reading experience that remembers your progress. Private, offline, no account required.

How RSVP Speed Reading Works

RSVP speed reading works by eliminating the back-and-forth eye movement that slows traditional reading down. Words appear one at a time in a fixed position, so your brain can process text faster with less effort.

Easy RSVP Book Reader also uses ORP, Optimal Recognition Point, which anchors each word at the exact character your eye naturally lands on first. The result is a reading experience that feels effortless and allows you to read at a speed you never thought you would.

Features

Distraction-Free by Design

The interface disappears while you read. No toolbars, no sidebars, nothing competing for your attention. Just the words of the book in front of you.

Load Your Books

Drop an EPUB, PDF, or plain text file directly into the app. It will be saved to your library automatically. Start reading and your progress is remembered for each book so you always pick up exactly where you left off.

Reading Speed

Read at any speed from 60 to 600 words per minute (WPM). Use the left and right arrow keys to adjust WPM by 10. Press shift and the right or left arrow keys to fine tune by 1 WPM.

Smart Pauses

The app slows down naturally at commas, sentences, paragraph breaks, and chapter headings. Reading never feels robotic.

Controls

The spacebar plays, pauses, and restarts reading. W and S jump forward or back a chapter in the book. A jumps back 25 words and D forward 25 words. The progress bar appears on hover and can be dragged to any point in the book.

Customization

Choose your background color, text color, ORP highlight color, font, and font size. All settings are saved between sessions.

Private and Offline

Your books never leave your Mac. No account, no internet connection, no tracking. Free forever.
